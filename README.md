# Virtual Caddy Launch

Create a premium, modern landing page for a golf technology product called Your Virtual Caddy.

The page should be extremely clean, minimal, and visually impressive. It should feel like a high-end technology product rather than a generic golf website.

Hero section

The hero should be the main focus of the page.

Headline:
Your Virtual Caddy

Use a large, bold, sophisticated typeface. Keep the headline short and prominent.

Underneath, add a very short supporting sentence that communicates the idea of having an intelligent caddy that helps golfers make better decisions on the course.

Add a prominent call-to-action button:
Get Started

The hero should also feature a large, beautiful golf-related image/visual. Ideally, show a premium aerial view of a golf hole or an elegant visualization of a golf course with subtle technology/data elements integrated into it.

The image should feel cinematic and sophisticated, not like a generic stock photo.

Design direction

Premium and minimalist

Modern golf + technology aesthetic

Lots of whitespace

Strong typography

Sophisticated dark/neutral color palette with subtle golf-green accents

Subtle gradients and shadows

Smooth animations

Very polished and responsive

Avoid excessive cards, icons, borders, and visual clutter

Avoid the generic "AI startup template" look

The design should feel like a product that could realistically launch as a serious startup

Layout

Keep the landing page relatively simple for now:

Minimal navigation bar with the logo/name "Your Virtual Caddy"

Large hero section with:

"Your Virtual Caddy"

Short supporting text

"Get Started" CTA

Large golf image/visual

A short section explaining the core concept

A second CTA near the bottom

Prioritize the visual quality of the hero above everything else.

Make the initial version feel expensive, sophisticated, and memorable rather than information-heavy.
